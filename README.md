# Supernova Neutrino Mass Ordering at DUNE via Quantum Entanglement


## Research Paper

**Exploring supernova neutrino mass ordering at DUNE via quantum entanglement**

- **arXiv:** [2602.04800](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04800)
- **Authors:** Adikiran Johny, Athulkrishna R, Rudra Majhi, and Suchismita Sahoo.

## Analysis Codes

- `analysis/event_rate_spectra_nh_vs_ih.ipynb` — Example notebook demonstrating event rate spectra as a function of reconstructed energy for Normal Hierarchy (NH) and Inverted Hierarchy (IH).

## Research Presentation

This repository also contains a presentation prepared for the **Neutrinos from Home** talk.

- **Presentation:** [Exploring Supernova Neutrino Mass Ordering at DUNE via Quantum Entanglement](presentations/Talk_Entanglement_Neutrino_from_Home.pdf)

The presentation discusses supernova neutrino oscillations, quantum entanglement, and the potential of DUNE to investigate neutrino mass ordering.
